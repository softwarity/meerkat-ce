# Documentation

Meerkat is an app gateway: one door in front of your internal applications,
which takes care of authentication, access rules, routing and observation, so
your services do not have to.

## Where to start

- **[What Meerkat is](/#/docs/start/what-is-meerkat)** - what the product does, in two minutes.
- **[Quick start](/#/docs/start/quick-start)** - a gateway running and a first application behind it.
- **[Concepts](/#/docs/concepts/architecture)** - the two planes, routes, identity, access.

## Reference

- **[Predicates](/#/docs/predicates/overview)** - the eleven ways a route decides a request is for it.
- **[Filters](/#/docs/filters/overview)** - the thirty-three ways it transforms one.
- **[The console](/#/docs/console/overview)** - every screen, and what it is for.

## Looking after it
