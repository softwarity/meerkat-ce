Plug

plug runs a developer's local process as a member of the cluster: their service answers under its real name, and reaches the others by theirs - no port-forward, no code change. A deployed service of the same name is parked for the session and put back after. Developers connect through a tunnel this gateway opens, each with their own key. Everything plug can do - profiles, several clusters at once, volumes, environments - is in plug's documentation.
