OpenTelemetry

A trace is the story of one request, step by step, with the time spent in each. The counters on the Metrics screen say something is wrong and how often; a trace says where the seconds went. Meerkat is the only component that can measure its own share of them.

Export to an OpenTelemetry collector

In both editions, a traceparent travels to your services and its identifier is in the access log - which is what joins a line of ours to your own audit. Turning this on sends what you choose below: Meerkat's own spans - the time it spent choosing a route and checking access, which is a gap in the trace otherwise - and its counters.

Detail the gateway's own work

Adds what Meerkat does inside each crossing to the traces it records: the identity handed to your service - who the caller is, and its signature - and every query to its store. Off, a crossing is two spans and the gateway's own time is one number; on, you see where it goes. It never adds a trace, only depth to the ones already sampled - each weighs more in your collector, which is why it stays off unless somebody is looking.
