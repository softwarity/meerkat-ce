Endpoints and token

{{ origin() }}/api/notifications

POST / Publish a notification. Returns its id.
GET / Your live notifications, filtered by meta.<key>.
DELETE /{id} Withdraw one. It leaves every reader's page at once.
DELETE /?meta.<key>=... Withdraw every one the filter matches.
GET /{id}/acks Who acknowledged it, and who has not yet.

With the Meerkat token mode, send an internal token with the perimeter Notifications only: Authorization: Bearer mk_.... You list, withdraw and follow only what you sent: publish and withdraw with the same credential.

This API is on the internal plane: a port inside the cluster, never exposed through an ingress. The credential a service must present is set under Access. Services publish the notifications; this console shows what was sent and who read it, and can withdraw it.

Publish

A person receives it only if they may open where it leads. The readers see it on their notifications page, with a count on the user button or the portal bar.

64 KiB at most. An unknown field is refused.

tenant A tenant id or name, optional when there is only one. Its notifications must be on. * sends a copy to every tenant (see below).
roles Only the members holding one of these roles. An unknown role is refused.
users Only these members, by username. With roles, either is enough. Without both, every member of the tenant. Someone who is not a member is refused.
title Required. Plain text, 200 characters at most, the same for every reader.
body Plain text, 4000 characters at most.
level info by default, or success, warning, error.
detail Where the notification leads: {{ "{" }}service, path{{ "}" }}, a screen behind this gateway, or an https {{ "{" }}url{{ "}" }}. Name the service as it is called in the cluster and the path as it sees it; Meerkat builds the public address. Only the people allowed to open it receive the notification.
actions Buttons, five at most: a label of 60 characters and {{ "{" }}service, path{{ "}" }} or an https {{ "{" }}url{{ "}" }}. The reader's browser makes the call, with their session. Without a detail, a person receives it if they may call one of the actions.
actions[].method GET by default, or POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE.
actions[].body JSON, sent as application/json; not with GET. The reader can see it: put no secret in it.
mail Also send it by mail. Refused when no mail relay is configured. A mail carries no action.
expires An ISO 8601 duration (P7D, PT12H) or an RFC 3339 date. 30 days by default, 90 at most. Then it is deleted.
key 200 characters at most. Sent again with the same key while it is live, in the same tenant: the answer is that notification, and nobody is told twice. The same key in two tenants gives two notifications.
meta Your own keys and text values, to find and withdraw your notifications. Never shown to the readers.
ack none by default. single: the first reader to acknowledge it closes it for everyone. all: every reader acknowledges their own. A reader cannot delete it while they owe it.
onAck Your service, {{ "{" }}service, path{{ "}" }} only, called at each acknowledgement (see below). Needs ack.
Every tenant at once

With "tenant": "*", each enabled tenant whose notifications are on gets its own copy, with its own id. A tenant where nobody may receive it is left out, and the answer says which and why. Named users are taken in the tenants they belong to.

A person in several of these tenants sees one notification and gets one mail. Sent again with the same key, the copies already live are answered and the missing ones published. No tenant received it: 422.

Responses
201 Published: its id and the number of recipients.
200 Its key is already live: the same notification, with "existing": true. Nobody is told again.
400 Malformed JSON, an unknown field, or more than 64 KiB.
422 Refused. error says why and names the field, for example notifications off for the tenant (and who turned them off), an unknown role, a link that is not https, nobody in the audience allowed to open it, or more people than the cap an administrator set.
401, 403 The credential is missing or invalid (401), or this caller is not admitted, or its token is for another API (403). See Access.
Find and withdraw

meta.<key>=<value> matches exactly, meta.<key>=~<expression> by regular expression; all conditions must match. Withdrawn, a notification leaves every reader's page and asks nobody for anything.

Acknowledgements

With ack, the reader presses Acknowledge. Your list shows where it stands (ackState, acknowledged, ackBy), and /acks names each person.

Answer 2xx. Anything else, or no answer, is tried twice more. complete is true on the acknowledgement that closes it: the first with single, the last with all.
