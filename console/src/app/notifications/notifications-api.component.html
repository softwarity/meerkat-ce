Endpoints and token

{{ origin() }}/api/notifications

POST / Publish a notification. Returns its id.
GET / Your live notifications, filtered by meta.<key>.
DELETE /{id} Withdraw one by its id. Its readers see it withdrawn at once.
DELETE /?meta.<key>=... Withdraw every one the filter matches.
GET /{id}/acks Who acknowledged it, and who has not yet.

With the Meerkat token mode, send an internal token with the perimeter Notifications only: Authorization: Bearer mk_.... You list, withdraw and follow only what you sent: publish and withdraw with the same credential.

This API is on the internal plane: a port inside the cluster, never exposed through an ingress. The credential a service must present is set under Access. Services publish the notifications; this console shows what was sent and who read it, and can withdraw it.

Publish

A person receives it only if they may open where it leads. The readers see it on their notifications page, with a count on the user button or the portal bar. The audience you name is kept: someone who joins the tenant, or is given a role, while it is live receives it then, told once for all they missed, with one digest mail.

64 KiB at most. An unknown field is refused.

id Required, a UUID of your choice: it lets you retry without duplicates, replace or withdraw the notification, and follow its acknowledgements and mails. 200 characters at most, letters, digits and - _ . :. It is scoped by the caller this API recognises (a token's name, a ServiceAccount, the workload behind the address). Sent again under an id you already used, before it expires, even after you withdrew or replaced it: the answer is that notification, nothing is created and nobody is told twice. Your actions and onAck receive this id.
tenant A tenant id or name, optional when there is only one. Its notifications must be on. * sends a copy to every tenant (see below).
roles Only the members holding one of these roles. An unknown role is refused.
users Only these members, by username. With roles, either is enough. Without both, every member of the tenant. Someone who is not a member is refused.
title Required. Plain text, 200 characters at most, the same for every reader.
body Plain text, 4000 characters at most.
level info by default, or success, warning, error.
exercise true for a message sent to practise. It keeps its realistic level and audience, and carries the label EXERCISE on the page, the snackbar, the desktop notification and the mail.
detail Where the notification leads: {{ "{" }}service, path{{ "}" }}, a screen behind this gateway, or an https {{ "{" }}url{{ "}" }}. Name the service as it is called in the cluster and the path as it sees it; Meerkat builds the public address. Only the people allowed to open it receive the notification.
actions Buttons, five at most: a label of 60 characters and {{ "{" }}service, path{{ "}" }} or an https {{ "{" }}url{{ "}" }}. The reader's browser makes the call, with their session. Without a detail, a person receives it if they may call one of the actions.
actions[].method GET by default, or POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE.
actions[].body JSON, sent as application/json; not with GET. The reader can see it: put no secret in it.
mail Also send it by mail. Refused when no mail relay is configured. A mail carries no action. The mails wait in the database and survive a restart; your list counts them by state in mails (see below).
expires An ISO 8601 duration (P7D, PT12H) or an RFC 3339 date. 30 days by default, 90 at most. Then it is deleted.
replaces The id of an earlier notification of yours. It ends as replaced when this one is published, and leaves its readers' list (see below). The replacement has an id of its own.
meta Your own keys and text values, to find and withdraw your notifications. Never shown to the readers.
ack none by default. single: the first reader to acknowledge it closes it for everyone. all: every reader acknowledges their own. A reader cannot delete it while they owe it.
onAck Your service, {{ "{" }}service, path{{ "}" }} only, called at each acknowledgement (see below). Needs ack.
Every tenant at once

With "tenant": "*", each enabled tenant whose notifications are on gets a copy, all under the one id. A tenant where nobody may receive it is left out, and the answer says which and why. Named users are taken in the tenants they belong to.

A person in several of these tenants sees one notification and gets one mail. Sent again with the same id, the copies already published are answered and the missing ones published. No tenant received it: 422.

Responses
201 Published: its id and the number of recipients.
200 Its id is already known: the same notification, with "existing": true, and state when it was withdrawn or replaced. Nobody is told again.
400 Malformed JSON, an unknown field, or more than 64 KiB.
422 Refused. error says why and names the field, for example notifications off for the tenant (and who turned them off), an unknown role, a link that is not https, nobody in the audience allowed to open it, or more people than the cap an administrator set.
401, 403 The credential is missing or invalid (401), or this caller is not admitted, or its token is for another API (403). See Access.
Find and withdraw

meta.<key>=<value> matches exactly, meta.<key>=~<expression> by regular expression; all conditions must match. Withdrawn, a notification stays on its readers' pages as a row saying it was withdrawn, and asks nobody for anything.

Replace and withdraw

Name an earlier notification of yours by its id. Replaced, it leaves its readers' list and count, the replacement standing in its place, and its mails not sent yet are cancelled. Withdrawn, it stays as a row saying it was withdrawn, with nothing of what it said, and its mails not sent yet are cancelled. An id sent to every tenant names every copy.

Your calls may arrive out of order. A replacement or a withdrawal that names an id not received yet is kept as long as a notification lives (30 days by default, 90 at most): when that id arrives, it is stored already ended, and nobody is told or mailed.

Mail

One mail per reader with an address, queued in the database and sent one at a time: a gateway that stops or restarts sends the rest later. A temporary refusal (4yz) is tried again for 24 hours, a permanent one (5yz) fails. Accepted means accepted by the relay, never delivered. A connection lost after the message was sent is unknown, and never sent again on its own. Every attempt carries the same Message-ID.

Acknowledgements

With ack, the reader presses Acknowledge. Your list shows where it stands (ackState, acknowledged, ackBy), and /acks names each person.

With onAck, Meerkat calls your service at each acknowledgement, through its route, on the application port:

Answer 2xx. Anything else, or no answer, is tried twice more. complete is true on the acknowledgement that closes it: the first with single, the last with all.
