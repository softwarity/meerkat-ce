The recommended path, and the only one where rotating costs nothing: a service reads this once, caches it, and reads it again the day a token names a key it does not know. Rotate, and it finds the new one on its own. A service that cannot fetch a JWKS takes the PEM from its row below instead - and then every rotation means going back to every service holding one, which is why that is the fallback and not the plan.
